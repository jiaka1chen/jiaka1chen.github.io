Research Interest

Foundation models and general-purpose agents bring internet-scale priors and deliberate reasoning, yet physical robots operate under strict real-time latency and safety constraints. My research aims to understand what representations transfer across humans and embodiments and where general-purpose agents belong in the robotics stack, connecting three stages into one closed loop:

  • Human & Cross-Embodiment Priors — While LLMs have unlocked internet-scale text, robotics remains bottlenecked by robot-data scarcity. This motivates structured, transferable representations that turn abundant human data and diverse embodiments into reusable physical and behavioral priors for robotic manipulation.
  • From Slow Agents to Fast Representations & Models — General-purpose agents can already orchestrate simulators, plan over long horizons, and even directly control robots, yet remain far too slow for real-time physical interaction. A central question is how to transform these deliberate capabilities into faster representations, models, and execution paradigms.
  • Agent-Driven Real-World Robotics Loops — Closing the loop in the physical world by empowering agents to drive end-to-end robotics research and engineering, autonomously conducting exploration, hardware rollouts, and systematic evaluation within explicit safety boundaries to continually feed grounded experience back into representations and models.

While my work so far has focused on the learning side, I am also very interested in getting hands-on with robot hardware design.

Prior to starting at UIUC in January 2026, I was a Ph.D. student in Finance at the University of Rochester, where I completed my doctoral coursework in good standing before choosing to exit with an M.S. degree at the end of 2025 to pivot full-time into AI and robotics. While my training at Rochester gave me a strong foundation in quantitative reasoning and principled problem formulation, I realized that rather than analyzing markets from the outside, I wanted to build intelligent systems that perceive, reason, and act in the physical world. This experience not only shaped my research perspective, but also gave me the clarity and conviction to pursue the problems that matter most to me.

Earlier, I received my bachelor’s degree in Financial Technology with a second major in Computer Science from the University of Hong Kong, where I built a foundation in quantitative analysis, computing, and machine learning.

arXiv preprint

Real2Worlds2Real Scaling Self-Improving Agentic Real2Sim2Real for Robot Manipulation

Real2Worlds2Real is an end-to-end agentic pipeline that connects simulation world building with manipulation policy learning. From a few real observations, the agent reconstructs a digital twin and generates diverse digital cousins to help policies generalize beyond the original scene. The agent improves over time by using simulation feedback to refine worlds and policies and distilling validated procedures into reusable skills for future tasks.

Pose estimation · Child-centric video pose modeling 2026

ChildPose: Foundation for Children Pose Modeling

Jiakai Chen, Yifan Shen, Boyi Li, Chuanmiao Dong, Xu Cao

Poster, NeurIPS 2026

ChildPose shows that pediatric pose estimation needs child-specific temporal and developmental modeling beyond fine-tuning adult-centric estimators. It combines memory-augmented temporal attention with age-aware co-training to produce stable video predictions, outperforming off-the-shelf and child-data fine-tuned baselines on unseen subjects.

Robot learning · Learning from human video 2026

V2AP: Learning Affordance Posteriors for Robotic Grasping from Human Video Priors

Yanghong Liu, Fan Xu, Zehao Lin, Jiakai Chen, Haoran Lu, Zhengtao Yao, Yubiao Zhang, Masayoshi Tomizuka, Wei Zhan, Ruihai Wu

Poster, RoboData Workshop @ RSS 2026

V2AP converts object-centric priors from human videos into robot-usable grasp supervision. It verifies candidate grasps in simulation, then aggregates successful rollouts into affordance posteriors that improve grasp success over raw human priors.
